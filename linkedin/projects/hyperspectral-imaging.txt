Hyperspectral Imaging Analysis

Establishing hyperspectral imaging capability from scratch: standing up the camera, learning the domain, and leaving behind a library and pipeline the team could actually use.

What I did:
Read the research on hyperspectral imaging for early disease indicators, set up the camera physically with measured positioning, and worked through lighting conditions until images were usable - the breakthrough being that calibration captures had been misread as needing extra brightness. Wrote a helper library on top of the camera SDK providing region-of-interest selection, cleaning and preprocessing for downstream analysis, plus a data-generation pipeline covering feature extraction, brightness extraction, and normalization against a dark reference.

Outcome:
Turned an unfamiliar instrument into a documented, repeatable capability - camera setup, helper library, data pipeline and written documentation for teams in other locations.

Tech: Python, Jupyter, Hyperspectral imaging, Spectral calibration, Feature extraction, NumPy, Data pipelines
